# GenderForm 1.0.0 — Dok_Si

- Four breast shapes: Standard, Rounded, Natural, Triangular.
- Standard maximum: 100%; all custom shapes maximum: 150%.
- Rounded/Natural geometry reduced from 6×6 to 5×5 surface subdivision for lower vertex/quad cost while preserving the rounded appearance.
- Triangular rendering is guarded against empty quad slots and no longer crashes on `quad.uvs`.
- Female hurt sound replaces the vanilla hurt sound instead of playing on top of it.
- Contributor/author nametag is disabled above players; credits remain available in the menu.
- Dok_Si credits mannequin uses the supplied `nick_Dok_Si.json` appearance settings.
